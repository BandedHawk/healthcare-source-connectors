Make corrections and retries revisions of their place

Step 2 of reprocess (STAGES.md §15.8.5, §15.8.10, DESIGN revision cn).

- Fail store states are now FAILED, REVISION_PENDING, DECLINED and
  SUPERSEDED. CORRECTION_PENDING, FAILED_CORRECTION, CORRECTED and
  RETRIED go; correct, retry and decline take a FAILED failure only.
- A correction or retry notes, when submitted, the revision it makes
  (the failed object's line with this stage's revision one more) and the
  failed record's ID (stage_correction: record_id, generation, line).
- Convert stage: a correction is claimed under its place's key and
  stored through the currency rule. Its failure is superseded whether or
  not it passes; a failed revision is a FAILED failure of its own. One
  overtaken by something newer is stored late and flagged, not refused.
  Its records row and record.stored carry the revision.
- Split stage: a piece's correction is its next split revision, on its
  row and record.split. A correction of the item as a whole keeps its
  rules until step 4 makes it a generation.
- Producer results only for an item's first report, in its first
  generation; no operator action sends the producer anything (§15.8.7).
- One-time move at start (CorrectionStore.moveStates, audited
  failure.states_moved): old states to new, with events; FAILED_CORRECTION
  gets its correction's failure as a FAILED row of the next revision,
  counted at the convert stage. Earlier events keep their state names.
- failures lists each failure's lineage. Lineage.stageOf and
  nextRevision added.
- Tests: the move, a correction overtaken by a newer revision, failed
  corrections and retries as failures of their own, results sent once.
  The stage tests' stage is now named convert.
- Operator READMEs of both stages updated.
- Javadoc for six members strict lint found without it.

Not yet tried on the sandbox.
